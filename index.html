THE MIND BEHIND THE ARCHIVE

Every mystery begins with an imagination.

Hello, I'm Rabia Sehar — a university student, creative thinker and the person behind The Midnight Archive.

I've always been interested in psychological mysteries, unusual characters, unexpected plot twists and fictional worlds that leave something to think about.

I enjoy exploring creative ideas and turning little thoughts into something meaningful. Alongside storytelling, I'm also studying computer science and learning how to create digital projects.

This website brings those interests together. It is my personal creative space to experiment, imagine, write and build something of my own.

With imagination, Rabia.
